# 🎓 Carrier Guide - Elite Higher Education Roadmap

An interactive, responsive, and visually stunning web application built with a **Unique Premium Gold & Obsidian Glassmorphism** theme. Designed specifically to guide high school students and parents through their 12th standard career pathways, degree options, expected salaries, duration in years, and top 5 best colleges.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **🔒 Step 1: Secure OTP Authentication Portal**
   - Candidate Name & 10-Digit Mobile Number validation (+91).
   - "Send OTP" button with real-time OTP generation (e.g. `8492`).
   - Copy / Auto-fill OTP button for instant seamless testing.
   - 4-Digit auto-advancing input boxes with backspace & paste support.
   - 30-Second resend countdown timer and validation.

2. **📚 Step 2: 12th Standard Course / Stream Selector**
   - **Computer Science Group (PCM + CS)**
   - **Biology & Maths Group (PCMB)**
   - **Pure Science Group (PCB / Botany & Zoology)**
   - **Commerce with Business Maths**
   - **Commerce with Computer Applications**
   - **Arts & Humanities**
   - **Vocational & Skill Streams (Aviation, Pilot, VFX, Design)**
   - Subject tags, stream descriptions, and interactive selection.

3. **💼 Step 3: Degree Programs, Duration & Salary Matrix**
   - Tailored degrees filtered specifically for the selected 12th course.
   - ⏳ **Duration**: (e.g., 4 Years, 5.5 Years, 3 Years).
   - 💰 **Salary Package Projections**: Entry Level, Mid-Level (3-6 yrs), and Senior/Highest CTC.
   - 🎯 **Career Roles**: Job profiles and industry scope.
   - 🔍 **Real-time Search Filter**: Instant search across degrees and keywords.
   - 🔖 **Shortlist**: Bookmark target degrees.

4. **🏛️ Step 4: Top 5 Best Colleges Modal**
   - Click "View Top 5 Colleges" on any degree.
   - Detailed Top 5 institutions (IIT Madras, AIIMS, CMC Vellore, CEG Anna University, Loyola College, SRCC, NID, etc.).
   - Ranking / Accreditation badges (NIRF Ranks, NAAC A++).
   - Placement stats, Average CTC, and direct official website links.

5. **🏆 Step 5: Summary & Thank You Section**
   - Candidate Career Dossier Report.
   - 📄 **Download / Print**: Clean printable PDF/print format.
   - 🔄 **Explore Another Stream** or Reset.
